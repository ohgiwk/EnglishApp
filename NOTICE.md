# Third-party data notice

The English–Japanese glosses in the original 1,000 entries of `src/data/vocabulary/v*.json` were selected and adapted from
[EJDict-hand](https://github.com/kujirahand/EJDict), which is dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0.

English word ranking was informed by the
[google-10000-english](https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english) frequency
list. The generated learning records, level grouping, categories, and example copy are
maintained as part of this application.

New vocabulary glosses and bilingual examples in the staged expansion are authored for this application. Editorial references and an aggregate public-exam comparison are documented in `docs/vocabulary-design.md`. Exam PDFs and extracted full text are not redistributed.
